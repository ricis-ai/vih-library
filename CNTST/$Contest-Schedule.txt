CONTESTS AND ON-AIR EVENTS — Tuesday, November 10 to Wednesday, December 9

Thirty days of contests and on-air events, in one place. Our net runs every Saturday. This file is replaced with a fresh edition as new dates are added. All times are 24-hour; contest times are Central with UTC in parentheses.

THIS WEEKEND

Visually Inconvenienced Hams Net — Saturday, November 14 at 19:00 Central, on the Blind Hams Bridge.

ARRL Sweepstakes Contest, CW — logs are due by 21:00 Central on Sunday, November 15 (03:00 UTC November 16). The contest itself ran Saturday, November 7 at 15:00 Central to Sunday, November 8 at 21:00 Central.

WAE DX Contest, RTTY — Friday, November 13 at 18:00 Central to Sunday, November 15 at 17:59 Central (00:00 UTC November 14 to 23:59 UTC November 15). RTTY on 80, 40, 20, 15, and 10 meters. Exchange: RST and a serial number. Single operators may operate up to 36 hours. Logs are due by 17:59 Central on Sunday, November 22 (23:59 UTC November 22).

JIDX Phone Contest — Saturday, November 14 at 01:00 Central to Sunday, November 15 at 07:00 Central (07:00 UTC November 14 to 13:00 UTC November 15). SSB on 160, 80, 40, 20, 15, and 10 meters, working Japanese stations. Exchange: signal report and your CQ zone number; Japanese stations send a prefecture number. Logs are due December 15.

OK/OM DX Contest, CW — Saturday, November 14 at 06:00 Central to Sunday, November 15 at 06:00 Central (12:00 UTC November 14 to 12:00 UTC November 15). CW on 160, 80, 40, 20, 15, and 10 meters, working Czech and Slovak stations. Exchange for stations outside OK and OM: RST and a serial number. Logs are due by 17:59 Central on Sunday, November 22 (23:59 UTC November 22).

SKCC Weekend Sprintathon — Saturday, November 14 at 06:00 Central to Sunday, November 15 at 18:00 Central (12:00 UTC November 14 to 24:00 UTC November 15). CW on 160, 80, 40, 20, 15, 10, and 6 meters, straight-key style. Exchange: RST, your state, province, or country, your name, and your SKCC number, or NONE if you are not a member. Logs are due by 17:59 Central on Sunday, November 22 (23:59 UTC November 22).

Also running this weekend: the PODXS 070 Club Triple Play Low Band Sprint, Friday, November 13 at 18:00 Central to Monday, November 16 at 17:59 Central, PSK31 on 160, 80, and 40 meters; the CQ-WE Contest, with sessions on Saturday, November 14 and Sunday, November 15; the Classic Exchange, Phone, with sessions starting Sunday, November 15 at 08:00 Central and Tuesday, November 17 at 08:00 Central; the first session of the AWA Bruce Kelley 1929 QSO Party, Saturday, November 14 at 17:00 Central to Sunday, November 15 at 21:00 Central; the Homebrew and Oldtime Equipment Party on Sunday, November 15, 07:00 to 09:00 Central on 40 meters and 09:00 to 11:00 Central on 80 meters; the Run for the Bacon QRP Contest on Sunday, November 15 at 17:00 to 19:00 Central; and the RSGB FT4 Contest on Monday, November 16 at 14:00 to 16:00 Central. Earlier this week: the RSGB 80m Autumn Series, SSB, on Wednesday, November 11 at 14:00 to 15:30 Central.
